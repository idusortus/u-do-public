You-Do — Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 22, 2026

You-Do ("the App") is a shared task management application developed by idusortus ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we handle it.

1. Data We Collect

Account information — When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo URL from Google. We store this to identify your account within the App.

User-generated content — Task titles, descriptions, group names, and invite codes you create are stored on our servers so they can be shared with your group members.

Push notification tokens — If you enable notifications, we store a device-specific push token so we can send you notifications about task activity in your groups. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

Crash and performance data — We use Sentry to collect crash reports and basic device information (device model, OS version, app version) to diagnose and fix bugs. No personally identifiable information is intentionally included in crash reports.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not collect your contacts or phone number.
  • We do not collect financial or payment information.
  • We do not serve ads or share data with advertisers.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Account info: To authenticate you and display your name to group members.
  • Tasks and groups: To provide the core functionality of the App — shared task lists.
  • Push tokens: To notify you when tasks are created, picked up, or completed in your groups.
  • Crash data: To identify and fix bugs.

4. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored in Supabase (hosted on AWS infrastructure). Crash data is stored in Sentry. Both services use industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest.

5. Data Sharing

We share your display name and task activity with other members of groups you join — that's the whole point of the App. Beyond that, we do not share your data with any third parties except the infrastructure providers listed above (Supabase, Sentry) who process data on our behalf.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. If you want your data deleted, contact us at the email below and we will remove your account and associated data within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
